### 4.1 `DEC-V1.10-001` — Evidence-Backed Directive Registry & Support Status (`QA-01` Resolution)

#### 4.1.1 Retraction & Correction of Earlier Over-Classification (`QA-01`)
- **What Changed from `902b9c3a`:**
  1. In `902b9c3a`, `noarchive` was listed as a universally active V1 supported rule without noting that Google Search Central moved `noarchive` to its **Unsupported rules** section after retiring the Google Search Cached link and `cache:` operator. Conversely, in `902b9c3a`, deprecated/unsupported directives (`noodp`, `noydir`, `nocache`) were grouped under `parse_error:unsupported_token` alongside syntax errors.
  2. **Critical Distinction Required by `QA-01`:** A directive that is **syntactically well-formed** (`^[a-z][a-z0-9_-]*$`) but **deprecated or unsupported by Google** is **NOT** a `parse_error` (malformed syntax). In `audit.RobotsDirectiveObservation` (`internal/audit/model.go`, lines 181–182), the data model explicitly provides **two separate fields**:
     - `UnsupportedTokens []string` — for syntactically well-formed tokens that are not supported in the V1 directive registry (e.g., `noodp`, `noydir`, `nocache`, `no-index`, `nofollows`).
     - `ParseErrors []string` — for **proven malformed syntax** (`invalid_delimiter`, `empty_token_segment`, `missing_parameter`, `unexpected_parameter`, `invalid_parameter_value`, `missing_content_attribute`, `meta_agent_prefix`).
  3. **Special Audit of `noarchive` (`CONFIRMED` vs `PLATFORM_SPECIFIC`):**
     - **Verified Fact 1 (`SRC-GOOGLE-ROBOTS-001`):** Google Search Central moved `noarchive` to the *"Unsupported rules"* section because Google retired cached links; Googlebot ignores `noarchive` as a no-op.
     - **Verified Fact 2 (`knowledge/02-audit-check-catalog.md`, line 614 — `INDEX-007`):** Our own authoritative check catalog explicitly states under `INDEX-007`: *"Lưu ý `noarchive` đã lỗi thời trên Google Search từ 2024 khi Google bỏ liên kết Cached, nhưng một số bot khác vẫn có thể đọc."*
     - **Verified Fact 3 (Bing Webmaster Guidelines):** Bing and several archiving/crawler systems still recognize `noarchive` (Bing honors it until March 1, 2027).
     - **Contract Decision on `noarchive`:** `noarchive` is classified in the V1 registry as **`VALID_LEGACY_CROSS_ENGINE` -> `PASS` in `AR-INDEX-003`** (valid syntax, recognized in the V1 directive registry per `knowledge/02-audit-check-catalog.md:614`, inert no-op on Google, active on other bots) to prevent false-positive `P1` warnings on multi-engine sites.

### 4.2 `DEC-V1.10-002` — `X-Robots-Tag` Parsing & User-Agent Prefix Disambiguation (`QA-03`, `OQ-2`, `OQ-3` Resolution)

#### 4.2.1 Closing `OQ-3` — HTML `<meta name>` Scope (Do Not Expand Frozen Rule Scope)
- **Closed Contract Rule (`OQ-3`):** `AR-INDEX-003` evaluates **only** `<meta>` elements whose trimmed case-insensitive `name` is `"robots"` or a recognized crawler user-agent identifier (`isRecognizedCrawlerAgent(name)`: `googlebot`, `googlebot-news`, `googlebot-image`, `googlebot-video`, `adsbot-google`, `adsbot-google-mobile`, `mediapartners-google`, `apis-google`, `google-extended`, `bingbot`, `adidxbot`, `msnbot`, `oai-searchbot`, `oaisearchbot`, `gptbot`, `chatgpt-user`, `claudebot`, `anthropic-ai`, `perplexitybot`, `applebot`, `applebot-extended`, `slurp`, `duckduckbot`, `baiduspider`, `yandex`, `facebookexternalhit`, `meta-externalagent`, `twitterbot`, `linkedinbot`, `bytespider`, `ccbot`). All other `<meta name="...">` tags are out of scope.
- **Agent Prefixes Inside `<meta content="...">`:** Per `SRC-GOOGLE-ROBOTS-001`, user-agent prefixes (`googlebot: noindex`) are valid **only** in `X-Robots-Tag` HTTP headers, never inside an HTML `<meta>` `content` attribute. Therefore, `<meta name="robots" content="googlebot: noindex">` is a **proven malformed directive (`WARNING` — `parse_error:meta_agent_prefix:googlebot: noindex`)**.

HTTP header acquisition, HTML meta extraction, and directive parsing/normalization are **independent evidence channels**. A failure or incompleteness in one channel is tracked separately from the others (`MISSING != FALSE`):

#### 4.3.3 Authoritative Non-`2xx` HTTP Verdict Semantics (`QA-06` & `DEC-V1.10-005` Resolution)

**Why Non-`2xx` Responses Must Not Be Blanket `NOT_APPLICABLE`:**
1. **Frozen Manifest Alignment (`knowledge/07-v1-atomic-rule-manifest.md`, lines 215–229):**
   - `AR-INDEX-003` specifies:
     - `preconditions`: `"meta robots or X-Robots-Tag exists."`
     - `WARNING`: `"malformed or unsupported token observed."`
     - `UNKNOWN`: `"raw directive unavailable."`
     - `NOT_APPLICABLE`: `"no directive exists."`
   - Unlike `AR-CANON-007` or `AR-DISC-005` (whose frozen preconditions explicitly state `"canonical target is 200"` or `"listed URL is 200"`), `AR-INDEX-003` has **no** `status == 200` precondition in the frozen manifest.
2. **Distinguishing Rule Applicability (`NOT_APPLICABLE`) from Evidence Acquisition Completeness (`UNKNOWN`):**
   - **`NOT_APPLICABLE` (`"no directive exists"`)** is a positive assertion that **zero** robots directives exist across all directive channels applicable to the resource.
   - **`UNKNOWN` (`"raw directive unavailable"`)** means that zero proven malformed/unsupported directives were observed in the available evidence, **and** at least one applicable directive channel could not be completely inspected (for example, because HTML `<meta>` extraction is skipped on `3xx` redirects and `4xx`/`5xx` HTML responses).
   - **`WARNING` (`"malformed or unsupported token observed"`)** means at least one independently valid `directive:*` bundle in the acquired evidence (such as an `X-Robots-Tag` header on a `301`, `404`, or `500` response) contains a proven `parse_error:*` or `unsupported_directive:*`.
3. **Preserving Initial `3xx` `X-Robots-Tag` Evidence in V1.10b (`fetch.go` & `adapter.go`):**
   - In `internal/sitecrawl/fetch.go`:
     - When `hop == 0` is a `3xx` redirect (`res.StatusCode >= 300 && res.StatusCode < 400`), V1.10b captures `res.Header.Values("X-Robots-Tag")` from `hop == 0` into `out.InitialXRobotsTags` (mapped to `Page.RawXRobotsTags` and `Page.XRobotsTagTotalCount` on the redirecting URL's `Page` when `len(res.Hops) > 0`), rather than overwriting the redirecting URL's headers with the final destination URL's headers!
     - In `internal/audit/adapter/adapter.go`, `processPageDirectives` is invoked for **every** crawled URL (`pr.status > 0`), not only `if isHTML`, so `X-Robots-Tag` headers on `301`/`302` redirects, `404`/`410`/`500`/`503` responses, and non-HTML resources (`application/pdf`, etc.) are always normalized into `directive:<run>:<url>:header:<idx>` bundles.

##### Complete Non-`2xx` Verdict Matrix (`QA-06`)

## 5. Formal Token & Parameter Grammar Specification

### 5.1 EBNF Grammar

```ebnf
(* HTML <meta> content attribute value *)
meta_content_value      = optional_ws                           (* vacuous: 0 tokens -> no bundle *)
                        | directive_list ;

(* Discrete HTTP X-Robots-Tag header field-value *)
x_robots_header_value   = optional_ws                           (* vacuous: 0 tokens -> no bundle *)
                        | [ agent_prefix , ":" ] , directive_list ;

agent_prefix            = rfc9110_token ;                       (* validated per Section 4.2.2 *)

directive_list          = directive_item , { "," , directive_item } ;

directive_item          = optional_ws , ( standalone_token | parameterized_token ) , optional_ws ;

standalone_token        = "all" | "none" | "index" | "noindex" | "follow" | "nofollow"
                        | "nosnippet" | "notranslate" | "noimageindex" | "indexifembedded"
                        | "noarchive" ;                         (* case-insensitive *)

parameterized_token     = max_snippet_token
                        | max_image_preview_token
                        | max_video_preview_token
                        | unavailable_after_token ;

max_snippet_token       = "max-snippet" , optional_ws , ":" , optional_ws , int_ge_neg1 ;
max_image_preview_token = "max-image-preview" , optional_ws , ":" , optional_ws , ( "none" | "standard" | "large" ) ;
max_video_preview_token = "max-video-preview" , optional_ws , ":" , optional_ws , int_ge_neg1 ;
unavailable_after_token = "unavailable_after" , optional_ws , ":" , optional_ws , valid_date_time ;

int_ge_neg1             = "-1" | ( digit , { digit } ) ;
```

### 5.2 Comma-Aware Directive Splitter (`splitDirectiveItems`)

To prevent RFC 1123 (`Mon, 02 Jan 2006 15:04:05 MST`) and RFC 850 (`Monday, 02-Jan-06 15:04:05 MST`) dates inside `unavailable_after` from being split across the post-weekday comma:
1. Split the directive list on `,` into raw segments `seg[0..k-1]`.
2. Iterate `i = 0 .. k-1`:
   - If `seg[i]` (trimmed, lowercased) matches `^unavailable_after\s*:\s*(mon|tue|wed|thu|fri|sat|sun|monday|tuesday|wednesday|thursday|friday|saturday|sunday)$` **and** `i + 1 < k`, merge `seg[i] + "," + seg[i+1]` into a single item and advance `i++`.
3. If any resulting item has `strings.TrimSpace(item) == ""` (when the original raw directive string was non-empty after trimming), record `parse_error:empty_token_segment`.

### 5.3 Accepted Date/Time Layouts for `unavailable_after`

A trimmed `[date/time]` string after `unavailable_after:` is valid if and only if it parses cleanly via `time.Parse` under one of these layouts:
- **ISO 8601 / RFC 3339:** `time.RFC3339`, `"2006-01-02T15:04:05Z"`, `"2006-01-02T15:04:05"`, `"2006-01-02 15:04:05Z07:00"`, `"2006-01-02 15:04:05 MST"`, `"2006-01-02 15:04:05"`, `"2006-01-02"`.
- **RFC 1123 / RFC 822 / Google Search Central Examples:** `time.RFC1123`, `time.RFC1123Z`, `time.RFC822`, `time.RFC822Z`, `"02 Jan 2006 15:04:05 MST"`, `"2 Jan 2006 15:04:05 MST"`, `"02 Jan 2006 15:04:05 -0700"`, `"2 Jan 2006 15:04:05 -0700"`, `"02-Jan-2006 15:04:05 MST"`, `"2-Jan-2006 15:04:05 MST"`, `"02-Jan-2006"`, `"02 Jan 2006"`.
- **RFC 850:** `time.RFC850`, `"Monday, 02-Jan-2006 15:04:05 MST"`, `"Monday, 2-Jan-2006 15:04:05 MST"`.

## 7. Authoritative Verdict Precedence & Bundle-Isolation Model (`AR-INDEX-003` — `QA-07` Resolution)

### 7.1 Definition of an Independently Valid Directive Bundle (`DEC-V1.10-006`)

To guarantee that:
1. **Unusable URL identity (`Tier A`)** always returns `UNKNOWN`,
2. **Invalid deciding evidence (`Tier B`)** within a corrupted/conflicting bundle can never produce a false `WARNING`,
3. **An independently proven malformed/unsupported bundle (`Tier C`)** always produces `WARNING` even if unrelated bundles conflict or unrelated channels (`html_extraction_status`, `http_header_status`, `http_status`, `content_type`, legacy HTML) are incomplete or conflicting,
4. **Incomplete, ambiguous, or conflicting evidence without a proven violation (`Tier D`)** always returns `UNKNOWN`, and
5. **Complete evidence (`Tier E`)** deterministically returns `NOT_APPLICABLE` (0 bundles) or `PASS` (`>= 1` bundles, all `"none"`),

each reconstructed `directive:*` bundle `b` (keyed by its explicit `"directive:*"` reference in `SourceEvidenceRefs`) is classified independently before URL-level completeness is evaluated:

- **Bundle Provenance & Integrity Check (`isBundleIndependentlyValid(b)`):**
  A bundle `b` is **independently valid** if and only if **all** of the following hold on `b`:
  1. **Explicit Bundle Identity:** Every observation in `b` carries the same `"directive:*"` reference in `SourceEvidenceRefs`. (Any directive observation lacking a `"directive:*"` reference in `SourceEvidenceRefs` is placed in `unreferencedObs` and is **not** part of any valid bundle.)
  2. **Single Non-Conflicting `directive_source`:** `len(b.sources) >= 1`, all observations in `b.sources` have the exact same trimmed value, and that value is either `"META"` or `"HTTP_HEADER"`.
  3. **Single Non-Conflicting `directive_raw`:** `len(b.raws) >= 1`, and all observations in `b.raws` have the exact same raw string value (`b.raws[i].Value == b.raws[0].Value`).
  4. **Single Non-Conflicting `robots_directive_tokens`:** `len(b.robotsTokens) >= 1`, and all observations in `b.robotsTokens` have the exact same trimmed value.
  5. **Single Non-Conflicting `robots_parse_errors`:** `len(b.parseErrors) >= 1`, all observations in `b.parseErrors` have the exact same non-empty trimmed value (`parseErrVal != ""`), and every comma-separated code in `parseErrVal` is either `"none"` or starts with `"parse_error:"`, `"unsupported_directive:"`, or `"ambiguous:"` (and `"none"` is not combined with error/ambiguous codes in the same string).
  6. **No Internal Conflict in Optional Bundle Fields:**
     - If `len(b.targets) > 1`, all observations in `b.targets` have the same trimmed value.
     - If `len(b.unsupportedTokens) > 1`, all observations in `b.unsupportedTokens` have the same trimmed value.
     - If `len(b.scopeUnknowns) > 0`, all observations in `b.scopeUnknowns` have the same trimmed value in `{"true", "false"}`.
     - If `len(b.tokens) > 1` (`directive_tokens`), all observations in `b.tokens` have the same trimmed value.

- **Classification of Each Bundle `b`:**
  - **`VALID_VIOLATION_BUNDLE` (Tier C — Proves `WARNING`):** `isBundleIndependentlyValid(b) == true` **AND** `b.parseErrVal` contains at least one `"parse_error:*"` or `"unsupported_directive:*"` code.
  - **`VALID_AMBIGUOUS_BUNDLE` (Tier D — Requires `UNKNOWN` if no `WARNING`):** `isBundleIndependentlyValid(b) == true`, `b` is not a `VALID_VIOLATION_BUNDLE`, **AND** (`b.parseErrVal` contains `"ambiguous:*"` **OR** `b.scopeUnknown == true`).
  - **`VALID_CLEAN_BUNDLE` (Tier E Candidate — Supports `PASS`):** `isBundleIndependentlyValid(b) == true`, `b.parseErrVal == "none"`, **AND** `b.scopeUnknown == false`.
  - **`INVALID_BUNDLE` (Tier B — Invalid/Conflicting Bundle Evidence):** `isBundleIndependentlyValid(b) == false` (missing required bundle field, conflicting values within `b`, or malformed `robots_parse_errors` value). An `INVALID_BUNDLE` **never** qualifies as a `VALID_VIOLATION_BUNDLE` (`Tier B` cannot trigger `WARNING`), and forces `UNKNOWN` at `P3` unless a separate `VALID_VIOLATION_BUNDLE` on the same URL triggers `P2` (`WARNING`).

## 8. URL-Level vs. Bundle-Level Conflict & Missing Evidence Rules (`QA-07`)

1. **Strict Enforcement of `MISSING != FALSE`:**
   - **Bundle level (`Tier B`):** If a `directive:*` bundle is missing `directive_source`, `directive_raw`, `robots_directive_tokens`, or `robots_parse_errors`, that bundle is classified as `INVALID_BUNDLE` (`isBundleIndependentlyValid(b) == false`). It can never prove `WARNING` (`P2`) and can never prove `PASS` (`P5`).
   - **Channel 1 level (`Tier D.3`):** If `http_header_status` is missing or `!= "complete"`, or `x_robots_sample_truncated` is missing or `!= "false"` (and no `VALID_VIOLATION_BUNDLE` triggers `P2`), `P3` returns **`UNKNOWN`**.
   - **Channel 2 level (`Tier D.4`):** On an HTML response (`isHTML == true`), if `html_extraction_status` is missing or `!= "complete"`, or if `robots_meta_sample_truncated` is missing or `!= "false"` (and no `VALID_VIOLATION_BUNDLE` triggers `P2`), `P3` returns **`UNKNOWN`**.
2. **Isolation Between Bundle-Level and URL-Level Conflicts (`DEC-V1.10-006`):**
   - **Unrelated Bundle Conflict + Valid Violation Bundle -> `WARNING` (`P2`):**
     Suppose `directive:run:1:meta:0` is a complete, non-conflicting bundle with `directive_raw = "no-index"` and `robots_parse_errors = "unsupported_directive:no-index"`, while `directive:run:1:meta:1` has conflicting `directive_raw` observations (`"nofollow"` vs `"nosnippet"`). Bundle 0 is an independently valid violation bundle (`VALID_VIOLATION_BUNDLE`), whereas Bundle 1 is an `INVALID_BUNDLE`. Under `P2`, Bundle 0 short-circuits and produces **`WARNING`** with Bundle 0's observations as `PRIMARY` evidence. Bundle 1's corruption does **not** mask Bundle 0's proven violation.
   - **Unrelated Bundle Conflict + Valid Clean Bundle (No Violation) -> `UNKNOWN` (`P3`):**
     Suppose `directive:run:1:meta:0` is a `VALID_CLEAN_BUNDLE` (`"noindex"`, `robots_parse_errors = "none"`), while `directive:run:1:meta:1` is an `INVALID_BUNDLE` (missing `robots_parse_errors` or conflicting `directive_raw`). Zero bundles qualify for `P2`, and `len(invalidBundles) == 1` triggers `P3` -> **`UNKNOWN`** (preventing a false `PASS`).
   - **Internal Conflict Within the Only Malformed Bundle -> `UNKNOWN` (`P3`):**
     Suppose `directive:run:1:meta:0` is the **only** bundle on the URL and has `robots_parse_errors = "parse_error:invalid_delimiter:noindex; nofollow"`, **but** its own `directive_raw` has two conflicting observations (`"noindex; nofollow"` vs `"noindex, nofollow"`) or its `robots_parse_errors` has two conflicting observations (`"none"` vs `"parse_error:..."`). Because `isBundleIndependentlyValid(b)` is `false`, Bundle 0 is classified as `INVALID_BUNDLE` (Tier B), does **not** trigger `P2`, and returns **`UNKNOWN`** at `P3` (preventing a false `WARNING` from corrupted deciding evidence).
   - **URL-Level Completeness Conflict + Valid Violation Bundle -> `WARNING` (`P2`):**
     If `url_identity` is valid (`P1` passes) and a `VALID_VIOLATION_BUNDLE` exists, conflicting URL-level completeness fields (`html_extraction_status`, `http_header_status`, `http_status`, `content_type`) do **not** suppress `P2` (`WARNING`), because the directive bundle itself is independently proven on `SubjectURL`.

## 9. Legacy Compatibility, Versioning & Regression Analysis

1. **`NormalizationVersion` Policy:**
   - **V1.10a / V1.10a.1 / V1.10a.2:** Remains `"v1.10.0"`.
   - **V1.10b (SiteCrawl acquisition only):** Remains `"v1.10.0"` if adapter output is untouched in V1.10b.
   - **V1.10c (Adapter normalization):** Bumps `NormalizationVersion` from `"v1.10.0"` to **`"v1.11.0"`** when `robots_parse_errors`, `directive_unsupported_tokens`, `http_header_status`, `robots_meta_sample_truncated`, and `x_robots_sample_truncated` are emitted.
2. **Legacy SQLite Fallback Behavior:**
   - For SQLite crawl runs created prior to V1.10b (`pr.page.RobotsMetaExtracted == false` and `len(pr.page.RawXRobotsTags) == 0`):
     - Adapter falls back to `pr.page.MetaTags`, `pr.page.MetaRobots` / `pr.metaRobots`, and `pr.page.XRobotsTag` / `pr.xRobots`.
     - If any legacy directive bundle is independently valid and contains a proven `parse_error:*` or `unsupported_directive:*`, `AR-INDEX-003` returns **`WARNING`** (`P2`).
     - Otherwise, on legacy HTML rows, because `RobotsMetaExtracted == false` (`html_extraction_status == "missing_legacy"` and `robots_meta_sample_truncated` is not `"false"`), `P3` returns **`UNKNOWN`** rather than a false `PASS` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
3. **Zero Regression on `AR-INDEX-001`, `AR-INDEX-002`, `AR-CANON-007`, and `AR-DISC-005`:**
   - Vacuous `<meta name="robots" content="">` and `<meta name="robots" content="   ">` (`HasContentAttr == true`, 0 tokens) do not emit synthetic `directive:*` bundles, preserving `len(directives) == 0` -> `NOT_APPLICABLE` in `AR-INDEX-002` and `effective_noindex = "false"` in `AR-INDEX-001` / `AR-CANON-007` / `AR-DISC-005`.
   - Missing-attribute `<meta name="robots">` (`HasContentAttr == false`) emits a `directive:*` bundle with `ParsedTokens = nil` (`directive_tokens = ""`) and `robots_parse_errors = "parse_error:missing_content_attribute"`; in `AR-INDEX-002` (`internal/audit/engine/index002.go`, lines 327–338), `directive_tokens = ""` has no `index`/`noindex` conflict and does not break `AR-INDEX-002`.
   - Non-HTML and non-`2xx` `X-Robots-Tag` processing in `processPageDirectives` emits `directive_*` and `robots_*` observations while keeping `effective_noindex`, `robots_meta_tokens`, and `x_robots_tokens` emission gated on `isHTML` (and existing scope rules) so `AR-INDEX-001`, `AR-INDEX-002`, and `AR-CANON-007` behavior is 100% unchanged.

## 11. Resolution of Outstanding Contract Questions (`OQ-1`, `OQ-2`, `OQ-3`)

All three open questions from V1.10a remain closed using primary standards and conservative V1 guardrails:

| Question ID | Question Summary | Final Resolution | Justification & Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`OQ-1` (CLOSED)** | Empty comma segments (`"noindex, , nofollow"`, `"noindex,"`, `","`) vs. empty meta declarations (`content=""`, `content="   "`, and missing `content`). | 1. `<meta name="robots" content="">` and `<meta name="robots" content="   ">` (`HasContentAttr == true`): **Vacuous 0-token declarations (`NOT_APPLICABLE` if sole on complete channels; `PASS` alongside valid directives; never `WARNING`)**.<br>2. `<meta name="robots">` (`HasContentAttr == false`): **`WARNING` (`parse_error:missing_content_attribute`)**.<br>3. Non-empty directive with empty comma slot (`"noindex, , nofollow"`, `"noindex,"`, `","`): **`WARNING` (`parse_error:empty_token_segment`)**. | WHATWG HTML § 4.2.5 requires `content` when `name` is present (`<meta name="robots">` without `content` is invalid HTML), while `content=""` is valid HTML with 0 tokens. Explicit commas with missing operands are malformed comma-separated lists. |
| **`OQ-2` (CLOSED)** | Unknown/custom user-agent prefixes in `X-Robots-Tag` (`X-Robots-Tag: custombot: noindex` and `X-Robots-Tag: foo: bar`). | **Return `UNKNOWN`** (`ambiguous:unverified_agent_prefix` for `custombot: noindex`; `ambiguous:unrecognized_colon_pair` for `foo: bar`) unless `<lhs>` is a known `max*`/`unavailable*`/`no*` directive typo or standalone directive with parameter (`WARNING`), or `<lhs>` is a recognized crawler with an invalid `<rhs>` (`googlebot: bar` -> `WARNING`), or `<rhs>` has a proven delimiter/parameter syntax error (`WARNING`). | `SRC-GOOGLE-ROBOTS-001` allows any crawler user-agent in `X-Robots-Tag`. | **`OQ-3` (CLOSED)** | Suspected `<meta name>` typos such as `<meta name="robot" content="noindex">`. | **Out of Scope for `AR-INDEX-003` (Retracted).** Do **not** inspect `<meta name="robot">` or other non-robots `<meta name>` attributes in `AR-INDEX-003`. | Preserves the frozen `AR-INDEX-003` manifest boundary (`preconditions: meta robots or X-Robots-Tag exists`).
